## Design Context

### Users
Promptual is for casual image browsers and people new to AI image generation. They use the app to browse AI-generated images, discover visual ideas, search by title, prompt, or tag, and save or share images they like. The experience should make exploration feel simple and inviting rather than technical.

### Brand Personality
Gallery-like, readable, approachable. The interface should feel like a calm mobile gallery that gives the artwork priority while helping beginners understand what they can do next.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use a clean, image-forward gallery presentation with strong readability, clear controls, and restrained decoration. Avoid interfaces that feel unreadable, ugly, cluttered, overly technical, or visually noisy. The app should not lean on generic AI-interface tropes such as neon dark mode, glowing gradients, glass effects, or decorative complexity.

### Design Principles
- Let artwork carry the experience; chrome should support browsing, not compete with images.
- Make discovery obvious: gallery, search/tags, random, and favorites should be immediately understandable.
- Keep text readable at mobile sizes, especially prompts, metadata, tags, and action labels.
- Use color to clarify state and action, not as decoration.
- Prioritize beginner confidence with explicit empty, loading, error, and action feedback states.
